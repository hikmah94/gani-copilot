# Data and evidence methodology

## Current coverage

The MVP covers nine document-backed Niger State approved annual budgets from 2018 through 2026. It includes structured budget aggregates, projects extracted from available records, and a searchable document catalogue. Coverage varies by source and year and must not be interpreted as a complete ledger of expenditure or delivery.

## Evidence classifications

- **High:** a structured record and directly relevant documentary passage agree.
- **Medium:** relevant public evidence exists but interpretation is required.
- **Requires verification:** evidence is partial, indirect, or conflicting.
- **Unable to verify:** no adequate indexed evidence exists.

These labels describe the quality of available evidence, not the model’s subjective confidence.

## Financial interpretation

Approved allocation, released amount, expenditure, procurement, and physical completion are distinct concepts. GANI does not infer one from another. Ratios and totals are calculated in application code from query results; the model is used to explain the result in citizen-friendly language.

## Source handling

Each indexed document can record its title, issuing authority, year, type, publication date, source URL, indexing date, page count, and processing status. Extracted projects may carry a source document, page, and reference. Missing metadata remains visibly missing rather than being filled with generated content.

## Community evidence

Community observations are useful signals but are not official public records. Reports remain pending until moderated, approved-only reports appear publicly, and no report changes a project’s official status.

## Adding or correcting data

Provide the issuing authority, original URL, publication date when available, the exact affected record, and the document page supporting the correction. Material corrections should be recorded in an audit log and re-indexed where semantic retrieval is affected.
