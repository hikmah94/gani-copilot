# Judging guide

## One-line pitch

GANI turns difficult public-budget records into searchable, understandable and independently verifiable civic information, then gives communities a safe, clearly labelled way to contribute observations.

## Challenge fit

GANI addresses **Transparency & Accountability** by helping citizens understand budgets, find local projects, scrutinise allocations, and reach the original record. Its community-observation flow also supports **Stability & Social Cohesion** by separating evidence from rumours and preventing citizen reports from being presented as official facts.

## Intended users

- Residents trying to understand projects near them.
- Journalists and fact-checkers tracing claims to public records.
- Civil-society organisations monitoring budgets and service delivery.
- Public officials seeking a clearer, reusable way to communicate published records.

## Suggested live demonstration

1. Search for “Bida hospital”.
2. Open the result and explain approved allocation versus released funds or delivery status.
3. Open its source evidence.
4. Ask a comparison or sector question and show the structured answer.
5. Open a document and show that document Q&A is explicitly scoped or unavailable until indexed.
6. Show community observations and their moderation boundary.
7. Close on `/future`, explaining how the same architecture can support other Nigerian states and African jurisdictions.

## What makes the approach distinctive

- Evidence is a product interaction, not a footnote.
- Deterministic tools calculate financial answers; AI explains rather than invents totals.
- Retrieval is document-scoped and validated against D1 metadata.
- Community knowledge is welcomed without being promoted to official truth.
- The platform works without forcing citizens to create accounts.

## Honest limitations

This is an invention-sprint proof of concept, not an audited spending register, procurement system, emergency-reporting service, or proof that an approved project was delivered. Document Q&A depends on successful passage indexing. These boundaries are visible in the application and documentation.
